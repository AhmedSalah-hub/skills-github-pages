Welcome to my personal technical blog!

As software engineers, we spend countless hours solving problems, debugging tricky edge cases, profiling database queries, and exploring architectural patterns. Often, the lessons we learn fade away unless they are recorded and shared.

### Why This Blog?

1. **Reinforcing Knowledge**: The best way to understand an engineering concept deeply is to explain it clearly to others.
2. **Documenting Architecture Decisions**: Tracking how applications evolve from simple console prototypes to complex data layers.
3. **Sharing Practical Solutions**: Focus on hands-on code examples in **C#**, **.NET 9**, **Entity Framework Core**, and **SQL Server**.

### What to Expect

On this blog, I will be writing about:
- **Modern C# Features**: Exploring language advancements, record types, pattern matching, and performance improvements in .NET 9.
- **Database Engineering with EF Core & SQL Server**: Index optimization, query execution plans, concurrency control, and migrations.
- **Clean Architecture & Design Patterns**: Decoupling domain logic from infrastructure to build testable, scalable enterprise backends.

Stay tuned for upcoming technical deep dives! If you have thoughts or feedback, feel free to connect with me on [GitHub](https://github.com/AhmedSalah-hub).
